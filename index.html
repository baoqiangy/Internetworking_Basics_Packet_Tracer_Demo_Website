How to use this site

These are live instructor demonstrations, not student assignments. Each page includes preparation, a narrated sequence, commands, expected observations, teaching prompts, and limitations. The pages are printable and may be opened locally without a web server.

Recommended workflow: prepare a clean starting .pkt file, rehearse once in the exact Packet Tracer version used in class, and save a completed version for rapid recovery during the demonstration.
